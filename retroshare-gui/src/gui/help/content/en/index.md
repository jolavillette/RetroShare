# RetroShare Manual

RetroShare is a friend-to-friend network: your node only connects to the
nodes of people you have added as friends, and every connection is encrypted.
On top of these connections it offers chat, mail, file sharing, channels,
forums and boards, with no server in the middle.

This manual describes every page of the application, with its buttons and
menus. Choose a topic in the tree on the left, or use the **Search** tab to
look for a word in all the pages.

Press **F1** anywhere in the main window, or click the **?** button at the
top of a page, to open the manual on the page you are looking at. The
**Manual** entry of the main window opens it where you left it. Press **Esc**
to close it.
