# Preferences

The Preferences page gathers the settings of RetroShare. It is made of a list
of sub-pages, one per subject.

A change is applied as soon as you make it: there is no **OK** or **Apply**
button and nothing to confirm when you leave the page. A few options say in
their label that they need a restart.

The settings belong to the node you are logged in with. They are saved in the
directory of that node, so another node on the same computer has its own
settings.

## Layout

- To open the page, click **Preferences** in the list of pages of the main
  window, or choose **Options** in the menu of the tray icon.
- On the left, the list of the sub-pages. Click a name to show that sub-page
  on the right. The separator between the list and the sub-page can be
  dragged.
- On the right, a header shows the icon and the name of the current sub-page,
  with a **?** button that shows the short help of the sub-page when it has
  one. The options are below the header.

## The sub-pages

- [General](settings-general.md): start-up, tray icon, auto login, idle time.
- [Node](settings-node.md): the identifiers of your node and profile, the
  export of your profile and of your friends list.
- [Network](settings-network.md): network mode, port, bandwidth limits, Tor
  and I2P, IP filters, relays.
- [People](settings-people.md): reputation of identities and how long banned
  identities are kept.
- [Chats](settings-chat.md): fonts, search, chat rooms, private chat, history
  and styles.
- [Mail](settings-mail.md): reading options, tags, who may send you distant
  messages.
- [Files](settings-files.md): shared, incoming and partials directories,
  transfer options.
- [Channels](settings-channels.md)
- [Forums](settings-forums.md)
- [Boards](settings-boards.md)
- [Notify](settings-notify.md)
- [Plugins](settings-plugins.md)
- [Appearance](settings-appearance.md)
- [Sound](settings-sound.md)
- [Permissions](settings-permissions.md)
- [JSON API](settings-jsonapi.md): only when RetroShare is built with the
  JSON API.
- [Webinterface](settings-webui.md): only when RetroShare is built with the
  web interface.
- One sub-page per plugin that has settings of its own. See
  [Plugins](plugins.md).
- [About](settings-about.md)
