# Getting started

RetroShare is a friend-to-friend network. Your computer runs a **node** that
connects only to the nodes of the people you have added as friends, and every
connection is encrypted. There is no central server: what you publish travels
from friend to friend.

Three notions are worth knowing from the start:

- Your **profile** is your key pair, protected by your password. It is what
  your friends accept when they add you.
- Your **node** is one installation of RetroShare that runs with this profile.
  You can have several nodes for one profile, for instance a desktop and a
  laptop.
- Your **identities** are the names under which you write in chat rooms,
  forums, channels, boards and mail. They are separate from your node: an
  identity can be a pseudonym that nobody, not even your friends, can tie to
  your profile.

On top of the connections to your friends, RetroShare offers several
services: [chats](chats.md), [mail](mail.md), [file sharing](files.md),
[channels](channels.md), [forums](forums.md) and [boards](boards.md).

## How to

### Start with RetroShare

1. **Create a profile.** The first start opens the profile creation dialog.
   Choose a name and a password, move the mouse until the **Go!** button
   appears, and click it. See [Profiles and nodes](start-profile.md).
2. **Exchange IDs with a friend.** The [Home](home.md) page shows your
   RetroShare ID. Send it to a friend, ask for theirs and paste it in the
   friend wizard. Both of you must add the other one. See
   [Adding friends](start-friends.md).
3. **Wait for the connection.** The first connection can take a few minutes.
   Your friend then appears as connected in the [Network](network.md) page.
4. **Create an identity.** A first identity that carries the name of your
   profile is created with the profile. Create others, signed or pseudonymous,
   in the [People](people.md) page.
5. **Join the conversations.** Enter chat rooms in [Chats](chats.md), and
   subscribe to [forums](forums.md), [channels](channels.md) and
   [boards](boards.md). The lists fill up progressively: content reaches you
   through your friends.

### Get help inside RetroShare

- Press **F1** to open this manual on the topic of the page that is displayed.
- Click the **?** button at the top right of a page to do the same. The first
  time you open a page, a short introduction to it is shown in a bubble;
  click the bubble to close it.
- The **Manual** entry, after the page buttons, opens the manual where you
  left it. **Esc** closes it.
