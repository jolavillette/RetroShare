# Mail

RetroShare has its own mail system, which never leaves the RetroShare network.
You can write to a friend node, or to an identity that belongs to someone you
are not connected to. The Mail page holds your folders, the list of messages
and the viewer. Messages are written in a separate window, the composer.

## How mail is delivered

- **To a friend node**: the message goes directly to the node of your friend,
  over the encrypted connection between you. It can only be handed over while
  your friend is connected. Until then it waits in your **Outbox**.
- **To an identity** (distant message): the message is encrypted for the
  recipient, signed with one of your own identities and relayed by
  intermediate nodes until it reaches its destination. Neither side learns the
  node of the other. Delivery can take a long time, since it needs both nodes
  to be online at some point, and the message stays in your **Outbox** until
  an acknowledgement of receipt comes back.

A message that has been delivered leaves the **Outbox** and is found in
**Sent**. A message that cannot be delivered stays in the **Outbox**
indefinitely. Whose distant messages you accept is set in the
[Mail preferences](settings-mail.md).

## Layout

- On the left, the **Compose** button, the list of folders, the **Quick
  View** list and the line **Total:** with the number of messages of the
  current folder.
- At the top right, the **Tags** button, the search field and the help button.
- Below, the list of messages, and under it the viewer that shows the selected
  message.

A message that you open in a tab appears next to the first tab, which always
holds the list.

### Folders

- **Inbox**: the messages you received. The number in brackets counts the new
  ones.
- **Outbox**: the messages that wait to be delivered.
- **Drafts**: the messages you saved without sending.
- **Sent**: the messages that were sent.
- **Trash**: the messages you removed. They stay there until you delete them
  or empty the trash.

### Quick View

A quick view filters the list of the current folder. Click a quick view to
apply it and click it again to remove it. Selecting a folder also removes it.

- **Starred**: the messages you gave a star.
- **System**: the messages generated by RetroShare: friend requests, friend
  recommendations and publish keys.
- **Spam**: the messages you marked as junk.
- **Attachment**: the messages that carry files.
- One entry for each tag: the messages that have this tag.

The number in brackets is the number of messages concerned.

### List of messages

The columns, from left to right:

- star: click the star of a message to set or remove it;
- paperclip: shown when the message carries files;
- **Subject**;
- read status: click the dot to switch between read and unread;
- **From**;
- **To**;
- junk: click to mark the message as junk or to clear the mark;
- **Date**;
- **Tags**.

The star, read status, **Tags** and junk columns are only shown in the
**Inbox**. Click a header to sort. Unread messages are written in bold. The
icon in front of the subject tells whether the message is new, read, replied
to or forwarded, and marks friend requests and friend recommendations.

Select a message to read it in the viewer. Double click a message to open it
in a new tab or in a new window, as set by **Open messages in** in the
[Mail preferences](settings-mail.md). Double click a draft to continue it in
the composer.

### Search field

Type one or more words to keep only the messages that contain them. Click the
magnifier to choose where to search: **Subject**, **From**, **To**, **Date**,
**Content**, **Tags** or **Attachments**.

## Buttons

### Mail page

- **Compose** (tooltip **New Message**): opens the composer on an empty
  message.
- **Tags**: opens the tags menu for the selected messages (see Context menus).
  Disabled when no message is selected.
- **?** (help button): opens the manual on this page. The first time you open
  the page, a short introduction to the page is shown in a bubble; click the
  bubble to close it.
- `Delete` key in the list: removes the selected messages, like **Remove
  Message**. `Shift+Delete` deletes them for good without going through the
  trash.

### Viewer

The header shows the subject, **From:**, **To:**, and **Cc:**, **Bcc:** and
**Tags:** when there are any. The sender is a link: click it to write to the
sender. When the recipients do not fit on the line, the small button on the
right shows how many **more** there are and unfolds them (tooltips **Show
more** and **Show less**).

- **Reply**: opens the composer with the sender as recipient, the subject
  prefixed with "Re:" and the original text quoted. You cannot reply to a
  notification generated by your own node.
- **Reply all**: the same, with all the recipients of the original message.
- **Forward**: opens the composer with the subject prefixed with "Fwd:", the
  original text and its files, and no recipient.
- **Delete**: removes the message, like **Remove Message** in the list.
- **More** opens a menu:
  - **View source** (`Ctrl+O`): shows the HTML source of the message.
  - **Save as**: saves the message as an HTML file.
  - **Print**: prints the message.
  - **Print Preview**: shows the message as it would be printed.
  - **Buttons Text Beside Icon** and **Buttons Icon only**: choose how the
    buttons of the viewer are drawn.

When a message contains pictures and they are not loaded automatically, a bar
says **The loading of embedded images is blocked.** The **Load images** button
shows them; its menu entry **Load images always for this message** remembers
the choice for this message.

When the message is a friend request, a bar tells you so. If it came from an
identity, the **Send Invite** button opens the composer on an answer that contains a
link to your own certificate.

### Recommended files

Under the text, the **Recommended Files** bar gives the number and total size
of the attached files and the **Message Size:**.

- Arrow button (tooltips **Show the attachment pane** and **Hide the
  attachment pane**): shows or hides the list of files, with the columns
  **File Name**, **Size** and **Hash**.
- **Download all** (tooltip **Download all Recommended Files**): adds every
  file to your downloads.

A file attached to a mail is not carried inside the mail. The mail carries a
reference to the file, which you then download from the sender through the
file transfer system. See [Downloads and uploads](files-transfers.md).

### Message window

A message opened in a window has its own toolbar and a **File** menu.

- **Compose** (tooltip **New Message**): opens the composer.
- **Display**: opens a menu with **Buttons Text Beside Icon** and **Buttons
  Icon Only**, which choose how the buttons of the window are drawn.
- **Tags**: the tags menu for this message.
- **File > Save As File**, **File > Print...**, **File > Print Preview...**:
  the same as in the **More** menu.
- **File > Quit** (`Ctrl+Q`): closes the window.

## Context menus

### List of messages

- **Open in a new window**, **Open in a new tab**: open the message. Enabled
  when exactly one message is selected.
- **Reply to Message**, **Reply to All**, **Forward Message**: as the buttons
  of the viewer. Enabled when exactly one message is selected.
- **Mark as read**: enabled when the selection holds unread messages.
- **Mark as unread**: enabled when the selection holds read messages.
- **Add Star**: checkable; gives a star to the selected messages or removes
  it.
- **Mark as Junk**: checkable; marks the selected messages as junk or clears
  the mark. Junk messages are found in the **Spam** quick view.
- **Tags**: the tags submenu (see below).
- **Edit**: on a draft, opens it in the composer.
- **Edit as new**: on any other message, opens a new message in the composer
  with the same subject, text, files and recipients.
- **Remove Message** or **Remove Messages**: moves the selected messages to
  the **Trash**. In the **Trash** they are deleted for good. In the
  **Outbox** RetroShare first warns that deleting is not recommended: the
  message is deleted for good, with no guarantee that it has not been
  delivered already.
- **Undelete**: in the **Trash** only. Puts the selected messages back in
  their folder.
- **Save As...**, **Print Preview**, **Print...**: act on the current message.
  Enabled when exactly one message is selected.
- **Show in People**: only when one message is selected and it was sent by an
  identity. Opens the [People](people.md) page on this identity.
- **New Message**: opens the composer.

### Tags menu

The same menu is behind the **Tags** buttons and in the context menu of the
list. A tag is a coloured label that you give to messages to sort them.

- **Remove All Tags**: removes every tag from the selected messages.
- One checkable entry for each tag: sets or removes this tag. The predefined
  tags come first, yours after a separator.
- **New tag ...**: opens the **New Tag** dialog and gives the new tag to the
  selected messages.

Tags are renamed, recoloured and deleted in the
[Mail preferences](settings-mail.md).

### List of folders

- **Empty trash**: on the **Trash** only. Deletes for good every message of
  the trash.

### Recommended files

- **Download**: adds the selected file to your downloads. A message tells you
  when you already have the file.
- **Download all**: adds every file of the message to your downloads.

## Dialogs

### Compose

The composer is a window with a menu bar, a toolbar, the address list on the
left, the recipients and the subject at the top, the text in the middle and
the **Recommended Files** at the bottom. The status bar shows the size of the
message and, for a distant message, the number of characters left. **Send**
is disabled when a distant message is too big.

Toolbar:

- **Send** (tooltip **Send this message now**): sends the message and closes
  the window. At least one recipient is needed.
- **Contacts** (tooltip **Toggle Contacts View**): shows or hides the address
  list.
- **Attach** (tooltip **Attach File**): lets you choose files to recommend.
  RetroShare hashes them, then lists them under **Recommended Files**. You can
  also drop files on the window.
- **Save** (tooltip **Save this message**): saves the message in **Drafts**.
- **Quote** (tooltip **Add Blockquote**): indents the current paragraph as a
  quotation, or removes the indentation.

Menus:

- **File > New**: empties the text, after asking whether to save it.
- **File > Open...**: loads an HTML or text file into the text.
- **File > Save**: saves the message in **Drafts**.
- **File > Save As File**: saves the text as an HTML file.
- **File > Save As Draft**: saves the message in **Drafts**.
- **File > Print...**: prints the text.
- **File > Export PDF...** (`Ctrl+D`): writes the text to a PDF file.
- **File > Quit** (`Ctrl+Q`): closes the window.
- **Edit > Undo**, **Edit > Redo**, **Edit > Cut**, **Edit > Copy**,
  **Edit > Paste**: act on the text.
- **View > Contacts Sidebar**: shows or hides the address list.
- **Insert > Image**: inserts a picture file in the text.
- **Insert > Horizontal Line**: inserts a horizontal line.

When you close the window with unsaved changes, RetroShare asks **Do you want
to save message to draft box?**.

### Compose: recipients

Each line of the recipients table has a selector, **To**, **Cc** or **Bcc**,
and a field for one recipient. A new empty line is added as soon as you fill
one.

- Type the first letters of a name and choose in the list that appears. A
  name that RetroShare does not recognise is written in red and is ignored
  when sending.
- A friend node is written as its name followed by **Node name & id:**. An
  identity is written as its name followed by **Distant identity:**. A group
  of friends can be typed by its name; the message then goes to every node of
  the group.
- **Bcc** recipients are not shown to the other recipients.

As soon as one recipient is an identity, the line **From:** appears: choose
the identity that signs the message and that receives the answers. A
reminder says that distant messages stay in your Outbox until an
acknowledgement of receipt has been received. Without an identity of your own
you cannot send to an identity: create one in [People](people.md) first.

With more than 20 recipients, a warning recommends using a forum instead.

**Subject:** holds the subject. **Tags:** shows the tags you gave to the
message with the tags button next to it; they are stored with your copy of the
message.

### Compose: address list

The **Address list:** panel offers the possible recipients.

- The selector at the top chooses what is listed: **All people** (all the
  identities you know), **My contacts** (the identities you marked as
  contacts) or **Friend Nodes**.
- The search field keeps the entries whose name contains the text.
- **To**, **Cc**, **Bcc**: add the selected entries to the recipients. A
  double click adds an entry as **To**.
- **Recommend this friend**: adds the selected friend nodes as **Cc**
  recipients, the selected identities as **To** recipients, and inserts in
  the text a recommendation with a link to the certificate of each selected
  friend node. Use it to introduce friends to each other.

Context menu of the list:

- **Mark all**, **Mark none**: select or unselect every entry.
- **Add to "To"**, **Add to "CC"**, **Add to "BCC"**, **Add as Recommend**:
  the same as the four buttons. Disabled when nothing is selected.
- **Details**: opens the details of the friend node or of the identity.
  Enabled when exactly one is selected.
- **Sort by state**: checkable; sorts the entries by connection state first,
  then by name.
- **Filter only connected**: checkable; hides the friend nodes that are not
  connected.

### Compose: formatting

The bar above the text holds, from left to right:

- the paragraph style: **Paragraph**, or **Heading 1** to **Heading 6**;
- the font and the font size (tooltip **Font size**);
- **Set Text color** and **Set Text background color**;
- **Increase font size** and **Decrease font size**;
- **Bold**, **Underline**, **Italic**;
- **Sets text font to code style**: switches the selection to a fixed-width
  font on a shaded background, and back;
- the list button, with a menu of list styles: **Bullet list (disc)**,
  **Bullet list (circle)**, **Bullet list (square)**, **Ordered list
  (decimal)**, **Ordered list (alpha lower)**, **Ordered list (alpha upper)**,
  **Ordered list (roman lower)**, **Ordered list (roman upper)**;
- **Alignment**, with a menu: **Left** (`Ctrl+L`), **Center** (`Ctrl+E`),
  **Right** (`Ctrl+R`), **Justify** (`Ctrl+J`);
- **Add an Image**: inserts a picture file in the text;
- the emoticon button: opens the emoticon panel.

The context menu of the text adds to the standard editing entries **Paste as
plain text**, **Spoiler** (hides the selected text behind a spoiler),
**Paste RetroShare Link** and **Paste my certificate link**, and on a picture
**Save image** and **Copy image**.

### Compose: recommended files

The list at the bottom has the columns **File Name**, **Size** and **Hash**,
and a check box in front of each file. Only the checked files are sent with
the message.

- **Paste RetroShare Link** (context menu): adds the files whose links are in
  the clipboard. Disabled when the clipboard holds no file link.

You do not need to share a file before recommending it: the recipient
downloads it from you. Your node must be online for that.

### New Tag

- **Name:**: the name of the tag. **OK** stays disabled while the name is
  empty or already used.
- **Choose color**: the colour in which the tag is written.

## How to

### Write to a friend

1. Click **Compose**.
2. In the address list, choose **Friend Nodes**, select your friend and click
   **To**.
3. Fill in the subject and the text, then click **Send**.
4. The message waits in the **Outbox** until your friend is connected, then
   moves to **Sent**.

### Write to someone who is not your friend

1. Click **Compose**.
2. In the address list, choose **All people**, find the identity with the
   search field and click **To**.
3. Check the identity shown after **From:**.
4. Write the message and click **Send**. It stays in the **Outbox** until the
   recipient has received it, which can take hours or days.

### Send a file by mail

1. In the composer, click **Attach** and choose the file.
2. Wait until the file is listed under **Recommended Files**, then send the
   message.
3. Stay online: the recipient downloads the file from your node.

### Keep a message for later

1. In the composer, click **Save**. The message goes to **Drafts**.
2. To continue, open **Drafts** and double click the message.

### Sort your mail with tags

1. Select one or more messages in the **Inbox**.
2. Click **Tags** and check a tag, or choose **New tag ...** to create one.
3. Click the tag in the **Quick View** list to see only these messages.
